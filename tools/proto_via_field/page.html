Prototype (branch proto/via-field-viewer, docs/research/fdtd-field-view.md). Metal is a perfect conductor in the solver; the conductor loss is a perturbation estimate from the tangential H at the metal surfaces. Fields within a cell of a metal edge are not resolved (edge singularities). Not part of kisi's product.
