## Goal
Close the loop simulation → measurement for the 3D references (`validation/via_ref`) and kisi's fast models:
- the same geometry measured, computed in kisi-fdtd, CST and kisi;
- known stackups (or stackups extracted from structures on the board);
- de-embedding with the same IEEE P370 2×thru method that `validation/fdtd/sparam.py` already implements.

## Connectors
Screw-on, solderless edge-launch connectors at the board edge, reusable across all coupons.
- **Decision (maintainer, 2026-10-30, on the RF group's advice): 2.92 mm (K), Samtec `292-J-P-EP-ST-EL-01`** –
  50 Ω jack, edge launch, compression mount, performance to 40 GHz, PCB thickness .040–.100 in (1.02–2.54 mm, option
  `-01`), two #1-72 socket-head screws with split washers, 0.5–0.8 in-lb.
  - Why: in stock at DigiKey (maintainer, 2026-10-30), also at Mouser, which the maintainer can order from; the thickness range
    covers every board (1.55–2.05 mm, the 2.0 mm 8-layer board included); solderless and reusable; intermates with
    3.5 mm and SMA cables (to their own limit).
  - Replaces the v2 choice, SMA Southwest Microwave `292-04A-14` (27 GHz, planned to 26.5 GHz; no distributor).
  - Quantity: 2 per 2-port coupon, 4 for the 4-port coupons (pairs, coupling); the connectors move between coupons.
    4 needed (about 52 USD each, Samtec's price 2026-10-30), a fifth as a spare.
  - All 2-port coupons have the same feed length per side (12.5 mm, 4-port 15 mm), so one 2×thru per board de-embeds
    them.
- **Footprint:** generated by `test_board/make_coupons.py` from parameters in `test_board/connectors.json`; the
  geometry follows Samtec's drawing 292-J-P-EP-ST-EL-01-X-MKT rev C (sheet 2: two plated screw holes Ø2.06 mm, 2.79 mm
  from the edge, 5.84 mm apart; ground pads 6.48 mm deep and 10.52 mm wide where the jaws clamp, top and bottom). Samtec
  calls it a general footprint, not optimised for electrical performance. **DRAFT** until checked. Samtec's drawing,
  KiCad footprint and STEP model are not in the repository (their terms forbid redistributing the models).
- **Launch:** Samtec's general signal pad (0.52 mm, 43.5 Ω on our 0.21 mm 7628 prepreg) is replaced by our launch
  inside the same ground pads: the pin (Ø0.18 mm, 0.74 mm beyond the edge) rests on the 50 Ω grounded coplanar launch
  (7628: 0.315 / gap 0.3 mm), which runs to the edge with a via fence (0.55 mm) to 6.48 mm. Tip, taper, pad width /
  length / gap, the ground cut under the pad and the fence are parameters (README section 2). First kisi-fdtd
  optimisation step (board side, the connector's coax not modelled): |S11| −21.0 dB at worst to 40 GHz; Samtec's
  0.52 mm pad −14.6 dB, its 0.23 mm tip −16.8 dB. CST cross-checks the whole transition with Samtec's STEP model.
- **Benchmark:** Samtec's RF Characterization Report for the part (series 292, report revision 2023-06-01, tested
  2023-03; fixture PCB-240MM-112613-SIG-0, four layers Isola I-Tera MT40, 50 Ω grounded coplanar trace, launch
  optimised with a full-wave solver; PNA N5227B, 10 MHz–40 GHz, Maury 2.92 mm mechanical calibration): one connector +
  500 mil trace |S11| −23 to −25 dB at worst (34–37 GHz), |S21| about −1 dB at 40 GHz; two connectors + 1 in trace
  −17 to −20.5 dB, −1.9 to −2.1 dB. Our target is |S11| below −20 dB to 40 GHz per launch; the FR-4 lines lose more
  (tan δ about 0.02 against 0.003 for MT40, [Isola](https://www.isola-group.com/pcb-laminates-prepreg/i-tera-mt40-rf-mw/)).

## Stackups
- **JLCPCB:** published impedance stackups.
  - The default 4-layer 1.6 mm stackup is JLC04161H-7628.
  - The LibreVNA uses a JLC 6-layer stack: 7628 prepreg 0.2104 mm, εr 4.4, from its board file.
  - Auto-generated stackup files for all JLC builds exist in KiCad / Altium / JSON form, generated, not checked:
    [gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups](https://github.com/gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups).
  - JLC's own data: [JLCPCB impedance calculator guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/User-Guide-to-the-JLCPCB-Impedance-Calculator).
- **Aisler:** 4-layer 1.6 mm. Core and prepreg vary with the stock available, so the thickness and impedance vary;
  exact values only from their sales team
  ([Aisler community](https://community.aisler.net/t/4-layers-1-6mm-70-m-stackup/5523)).
  - So the Aisler board needs extraction structures (below), and a micro-section or the delivered stackup
    report.
- **Both boards:** every board carries the stackup on the panel and its own line structures, from which εr and
  tanδ are extracted. The 3D runs then use the extracted values.

## Data flow
1. **Coupon generator:** `test_board/make_coupons.py` (reuses `via_cases.py` for the 3D cases): launches, feed
   lengths, stepped board outline, KiCad PCB with zones, vias, edge cuts and connector footprints, one description for
   both the board and the 3D cases.
2. **kisi-fdtd:** the coupon with the launch, and de-embedded results of the same coupon.
3. **CST:** import the KiCad / ODB++ files with the stackup, waveguide or discrete ports at the connector
   reference planes, the same de-embedding. Alternative: the VBA macro generator planned in
   `via_references.md`.
4. **kisi:** the board file directly (`kisi net … --s2p/--s4p`).
5. **Measurement:**
   - Calibration at the connector reference plane (SOLT or ECal).
   - P370 2×thru de-embedding to the via, with `sparam.py`.
   - Mixed mode for the pairs.
6. **Comparison:** the metrics of `via_references.md` section 6, in `references.html` as a third source
   (measurement).
