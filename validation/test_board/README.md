# Measurement coupons (test boards)

Status: **prepared, not ordered.** The boards are generated, kisi reads them and their impedances are on target
(field solver), the 3D cases are written and one of them was run. **KiCad is not installed in the workspace where
they were made: DRC has not been run.** The end-launch connector footprint is a **placeholder** (section 3) and must be
checked against the vendor drawing before ordering. The plan behind this is `../test_board.md`.

Goal: close the loop simulation → measurement for kisi's fast models and the 3D references (`../via_ref`). The same
coupon geometry is measured, computed with kisi-fdtd (cases generated from the same description as the KiCad board)
and with kisi (the board file itself), and all three are de-embedded the same way (IEEE P370-style 2×thru,
`../fdtd/sparam.py`).

## 3. Connector and launch

- **Southwest Microwave end launch, narrow block (.350 in)**, clamp-on, solderless (pin interference fit):
  `292-04A-14` (SuperSMA jack, 27 GHz) or `1092-01A-14` (2.92 mm jack, 40 GHz; mates with SMA and 3.5 mm), both for
  boards .040–.090 in ([product list](https://mpd.southwestmicrowave.com/product-category/end-launch-connectors),
  [catalog](https://mpd.southwestmicrowave.com/wp-content/uploads/End-Launch-Connectors-2024.pdf)). The `-9`
  variants take only up to .065 in (1.65 mm), which a 1.6 mm board at +10 % exceeds. Pin .010 in (0.254 mm). The part
  `292-04A-5` named in the plan is not in the current list.
- **Recommendation: 2.92 mm (`1092-01A-14`)**: the stub coupons (VLV_5, BS, DIFF_SL) have their stub notch near
  24–30 GHz (kisi: 24.5 GHz quarter-wave estimate), above the SMA limit; the PNA-X can use 40 GHz.
- **Footprint: PLACEHOLDER.** The ".350 Transition Block PCB Footprint" drawing in the catalog is an image the
  workspace could not read, and the product pages could not be fetched. Signal pad, ground-contact mask opening, two
  NPTH Ø2.0 mounting holes at 4.5 mm from the edge / ±3.0 mm and the block outline are **guesses** that stay inside the
  8.89 mm block (`connectors.json`, `footprint.verified: false`). Replace them from the drawing or ask Southwest for a
  launch design (they offer launch designs and HFSS models), then regenerate.
- **Launch on the board:** a grounded coplanar section of 3 mm at the edge (width 0.315 mm, gap 0.3 mm, 50 Ω by the
  field solver) with two rows of fence vias (0.8 mm pitch) into the ground pour the connector clamps on, then the
  50 Ω microstrip (0.345 mm). Copper ends 0.2 mm before the routed edge (JLC rule). Connectors for bottom-layer feeds
  are mounted upside down on the same footprint (B.Cu).
- **Layout:** 2-port coupons are rows of 12.7 mm, one connector per edge, connected one row at a time. 4-port
  coupons have two connectors per edge at 12.7 mm (3.8 mm between the blocks, about 3.5 mm between two 8 mm coupling
  nuts: **check torque-wrench access** with the real cables; `--pair-pitch 15` regenerates with more room). Every
  2-port coupon is 25 mm edge to edge with its reference plane 12.5 mm from each edge, so one 2×thru per feed layer
  de-embeds all of them; the 4-port coupons use 15 mm per side (room for the fan-in) and their own pair 2×thru.
- **Price:** not published (no distributor price found on 2026-10-22); quote from Southwest Microwave.
  *Estimate: on the order of 100–300 USD per connector — not quoted.* Needed: 4 (for the 4-port coupons; they are
  moved between coupons).

## 5. Trace widths (from kisi)

Widths are solved with kisi on a probe board per cross-section (the same KiCad writer as the coupons; 20 mm line,
planes as filled zones, mask in the stackup): geometric bisection with the native formulas, then secant steps with the
2D field solver of `kisi.mjs` (`--solver auto`), rounded to 5 µm. The impedance of every rounded width with both solvers
(`boards/<stackup>.design.json`):

| Cross-section (JLC 7628 stacks) | w / s or gap (mm) | kisi formula | kisi field solver |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 Ω microstrip L1 / bottom | 0.345 | 50.9 Ω (+1.8 %) | 49.8 Ω (−0.3 %) |
| launch, grounded coplanar | 0.315 / gap 0.3 | 52.8 Ω (+5.7 %) | 50.0 Ω |
| CPWG coupon | 0.28 / gap 0.18 | 53.5 Ω (+6.9 %) | 49.7 Ω (−0.5 %) |
| 100 Ω edge-coupled microstrip | 0.205 / 0.2 | 104.1 Ω (+4.1 %) | 100.1 Ω |
| pair at the 1.0 mm via pitch | 0.315 / 0.685 | 104.3 Ω (+4.3 %) | 99.8 Ω |
| 50 Ω stripline In2 / In3 (6 layers) | 0.405 | 50.1 Ω | 49.9 Ω |
| 100 Ω stripline pair In2 (6 layers) | 0.19 / 0.2 | 110.3 Ω (+10.3 %) | 99.8 Ω |

The field solver is within 1.6 % of HFSS (`../fdtd/TESTS.md`), so it sets the widths; the formula deviations are
kisi's known weak spots (coplanar, stripline pairs) and are themselves a target of the measurement. The LibreVNA CPWG
width 0.35 mm would give 45 Ω (field) here with mask and 35 µm copper, hence 0.28 mm at the same gap.

- The stackup, mask and the `.kisi.json` (values with sources) are read; `config-check` reports no errors or warnings; `gaps` reports only
  the missing power tree (irrelevant here).
- With the field solver, what is outside ±5 % are the short fan-in / taper pieces next to coupled sections (uncoupled
  narrow traces, up to 127 / 137 Ω for 0.6 mm) and the trace pieces over antipads. With the formulas, the launch
  (+5.7 %), the CPWG (+7 %) and the stripline pair (+10 %) fall out (table above).
- **`check --all` returns no rows:** kisi checks only ic–ic and ic–connector lanes (`kisi-core/src/service.rs`,
  "ic-ic" | "ic-connector"); every coupon lane runs from connector to connector. `interfaces` lists them all with the
  right profile (se50, diff100). The coupons are therefore judged with `net` (impedance, loss, TDR, vias, mixed mode,
  crosstalk). Not changed in this branch; a possible enhancement: evaluate connector–connector lanes (board share:
  loss, return loss, skew) in `check`.
- What kisi flags, as intended: D_NONE (4 and 6 layers) "reference plane changes, no stitching via within 5 mm" (return
  path "missing"); the stubbed inner-layer vias of BS_L3L4 "stub 0.88 mm, quarter-wave resonance 24.5 GHz inside the
  analysed band".
- Via results (ladder model): A0800 / A1000 / A1200 38.9 / 42.2 / 44.9 Ω; D 48.2 → 54.2 → 69.7 Ω for 1 / 2 / 3.5 mm;
  pair vias Zvia 72–76 Ω (TDR minimum 90–93 Ω); VLV_5 via 23.6 Ω with IL −14 dB at 25 GHz (stub).
- XT_MS: kisi NEXT 3.8 % (formula) / 5.0 % (field), FEXT 15.0 % / 15.1 % (35 ps).
- The cavity net (CAV) is followed only as a trace ending at a via ("net splits into 2 parts"); kisi's board pipeline

## 7. 3D cases (kisi-fdtd)

Each coupon has one or more **windows**: the coupon (traces, vias, antipads, its own pours, the row walls) between two
port planes, on the real stackup, with lumped 50 Ω ports where the traces cross the window edge, planes into the PML,
the solder mask as a 0.0305 mm dielectric layer on both sides, zero-thickness copper widened by Wheeler's Δw for the
copper thickness (0.035 mm outer: +0.039 mm), thirds rule, 0.035 mm fine mesh, λ/20 at 40 GHz (G: 0.1 mm, 6 GHz). The
windows of the 2×thru coupons (`THRU_* w10`, `PAIR_* w6`) are the 3D fixtures, so the 3D results are de-embedded with
the same geometry as the measurement. Windows: `w10` ±5 mm around the via, `w18` ±9 mm (D_NONE: the walls are the
return path), `w6` ±3 mm (pairs), `w15` the via–line–via, `wc` the whole straight coupled section, `w10`/`w20` line
pairs (line–line), `w60` the whole cavity.

58 cases, 0.48–1.68 M cells each, 57.6 M cells in total (`fdtd_cells` in the manifests). The one case run here
(`kisi-coupons-jlc4-a__THRU_L1__w10`, 0.75 M cells, 4201 steps, 36–85 s on one shared core) gives S11 < −23 dB to 40 GHz;
de-embedding it with itself returns |S21| = 1.0000. *Estimate for all: 1–5 min per via case, longer for G (ringing);
a few CPU hours in total.*

```
cargo build --release -p kisi-fdtd
python3 validation/fdtd/run_refs.py validation/test_board/fdtd/jlc4_a validation/test_board/fdtd_ref --exe target/release/kisi-fdtd --jobs 2
```

Known model limits (`../fdtd/README.md`): lossless metal (no conductor loss, no roughness) and dielectric loss as a
conductivity fixed at `f_loss` (= fmax/2 = 20 GHz: tan δ is exact there, the loss is frequency-independent below). So
**compare losses with kisi** (Djordjevic-Sarkar, Huray) and use kisi-fdtd for reflections, phase, resonances and the
via alone (short, so loss hardly matters). The lumped ports add a low-frequency TDR offset (`TESTS.md` section 3); it
cancels in the de-embedding.

## 9. Comparison

`compare_measured.py boards/<board>.manifest.json MEASURED_DIR [--fdtd-ref fdtd_ref] [--kisi KISI | --kisi-mjs kisi.mjs]`
reads `MEASURED_DIR/<COUPON>.s2p/.s4p` (port order = the connector order in the manifest: 2-port 1 = left; 4-port 1 = P/A
left, 2 = N/V left, 3 = P/A right, 4 = N/V right), runs `kisi net … --s2p/--s4p` (reordering kisi's ports to the same
connectors) and reads the kisi-fdtd results, de-embeds all three with their own 2×thrus and prints per coupon:

- vias (A, B, D, VLV): TDR min / max (25 ps, 80 ps window), |S21| at 5–40 GHz, stub notch, delay; against the
  measurement: max Δ|S21| up to 20 GHz (or 0.8 × the notch), TDR errors, notch error;
- lines (LINE, CPWG_50, SL): εeff, loss per mm at 10 / 20 GHz (line–line), Zc of the de-embedded line, stripline TDR
  plateau;
- pairs (E): Zdiff min / max, Sdd21, Scd21 after the per-port shift;

## 11. To check by a human before ordering

1. **Connector footprint** (blocker): mounting holes, ground-contact area, block depth and whether Southwest wants
   copper closer to the edge than 0.2 mm; edit `connectors.json`, regenerate.
2. **KiCad**: open each board in KiCad 8 or 9, refill zones (B), run DRC, look at every layer and at the Gerbers
   (antipads on the planes, removed non-functional pads, no copper in the launch clearance). Then run

3. JLC: the templates still exist in the order form; impedance-control option and line specs accepted; inner layers
   In2/In3 of the 6-layer boards are nearly empty (decline copper thieving near the coupons if JLC proposes it).

5. SMA or 2.92 mm.
