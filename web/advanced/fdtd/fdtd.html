    S-parameters

    Metal is a perfect conductor in the solver; the conductor loss is a perturbation estimate from the tangential H at the metal surfaces, and the dielectric loss at other frequencies than floss is scaled (estimate). Fields within a cell of a metal edge are not resolved (edge singularities). The S-parameters are those of the cut-out with its feeds and lumped 50 Ω ports, not de-embedded.
